logProgress

Open-Source App to Track Finances, Health, and Daily Habits

Easily monitor your finances, health, and habits, set goals, and stay motivated.
Your data is stored locally on your device.

Install logProgress App

This is a Progressive Web App (PWA) — a web app that works like a regular app but doesn't need to be downloaded from an app store. You can install it directly to your home screen!

For Desktop Users (Chrome/Edge)

Google Chrome: Open the menu (three vertical dots) at the top right of your browser. Select "Install logProgress" to add it to your desktop.

Microsoft Edge: Open the menu (three horizontal dots) at the top right, and choose "Apps" > "Install logProgress".

For Mobile Users (Android/iOS)

Android (Chrome): Tap the three dots in the upper-right corner and select "Add to Home screen."

iOS (Safari): Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow) at the bottom, then choose "Add to Home Screen."

Once it's installed, you can launch it directly from your home screen—just like any other app!
